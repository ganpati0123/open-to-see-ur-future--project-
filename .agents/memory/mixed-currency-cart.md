---
name: Mixed-currency cart totals
description: How the store handles rupee-priced uploads alongside legacy dollar-priced catalog items.
---

Keep INR and USD subtotals separate unless the owner explicitly supplies an exchange rate and approves a unified checkout total.

**Why:** The uploaded crystal and bracelet prices are owner-specified in INR, while existing catalog items are priced in USD; adding those amounts together would mislead customers.

**How to apply:** Group totals by currency and label shipping policies clearly whenever catalog items use different currencies.
